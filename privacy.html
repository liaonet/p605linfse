Privacy policy — zen-gog

zen-gog is a personal-use application. It accesses Google user data (Gmail and Google Drive) only in Google accounts owned and operated by Artergy.com Limited and its director, through Google's OAuth authorisation, and only for the account owner's own purposes.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred to any other service. Access tokens are stored on the operator's own computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page.

zen-gog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
